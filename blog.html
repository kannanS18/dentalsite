Why Chai and Filter Coffee Stain Teeth Yellow — And How Laser Whitening Fixes It.

In India, daily consumption of hot black tea and filter coffee causes dark organic compounds called tannins to bond tightly to the microscopic pores of your tooth enamel. Over the years, regular brushing cannot remove these deep stains.

Why Home Charcoal Hacks Are Dangerous:

Rubbing abrasive charcoal powders or lemon juice scrapes away your natural enamel layer. Once enamel is lost, it never grows back and exposes yellow dentin underneath!

The Safe Clinical Method:

Philips Zoom cold LED laser safely dissolves the stain bonds deep inside the enamel in 45 minutes, lifting up to 8 shades while protecting enamel strength.
