Aesthetic Dentistry & Bleaching

In-Office Laser Vital Bleaching (Philips Zoom Power Bleaching)

Eliminates recalcitrant chromogenic stains caused by tea, coffee, tobacco, and dietary fluorosis. Our dental-grade Philips Zoom cold LED photo-activates a neutral-pH hydrogen peroxide gel to oxidize organic stain molecules without compromising enamel micro-hardness.

Duration: 45 Minutes
Shade Lift: Up to 8 VITA Shades
Gingival Barrier: Light-Cured Dam
Specialist: Dr. Rohan Mehra, MDS
Hospital Tariff
₹8,500

Includes pre-treatment ultrasonic scaling, amorphous calcium phosphate (ACP) desensitizing application, and post-operative shade certification.

Micro-Endodontic Therapy

Single-Sitting Micro-Endodontic Therapy (Molar RCT)

Biomechanical debridement and 3D hermetic sealing of infected dental pulp. Utilizing Carl Zeiss operating microscopes and continuous apex locators, our AIIMS endodontists preserve natural root architecture and eliminate odontogenic pain in a single clinical session.

Duration: 1 Visit (60 Min)
Anaesthesia: Computerized Wand System
Magnification: Zeiss 24x Optics
Specialist: Dr. Ananya Sharma (AIIMS)
Hospital Tariff
₹5,000 / molar

Includes digital radiovisiography (RVG), rotary nickel-titanium canal shaping, ultrasonic canal disinfection, and warm thermoplastic gutta-percha obturation.

Guided Implantology & Prosthetics

Endosseous Titanium Dental Implant (Nobel Biocare)

The global gold standard biocompatible tooth root replacement. Pure medical-grade titanium fixture achieves direct osseointegration into alveolar bone, restoring 100% of masticatory force and preventing residual alveolar ridge resorption.

Warranty: Global Authentic Passport
Navigation: 3D CBCT Surgical Guide
Biomaterial: Grade IV Cold-Worked Titanium
Specialist: Dr. Vikramaditya Sen (PGIMER)
Hospital Tariff
₹32,000 / fixture

Includes 3D CBCT treatment planning, computer-milled surgical guide, surgical sterile pack, and Nobel Biocare authentic traceability serial card.
